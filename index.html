    Rules that matter

    • 4
      4 women on the field, always.Max 3 men in a 7-player lineup.
    • W
      Only women run the ball past the line.Handoffs, pitches and QB runs go to women. A man can run only after he catches a pass past the line.
    • ≠
      No man-to-man pass twice in a row.After a man completes a pass to a man, the next completion needs a woman throwing or catching. A woman's run doesn't reset it. Call Pizza Slice.
    • 9
      Woman throws or scores the TD = 9 points.A regular TD is 6.
    • QB
      QB lines up 2+ yards behind the center.Throw from behind the line. One forward pass per play.
    • 1s
      Everyone freezes 1 second before the snap.Only one player can be moving at the snap, and only sideways or backward.
    • ⊥
      Blocking = screen only.Stand in their way, hands behind your back, no contact.
    • ✋
      Don't guard your flags.No hands, arms or ball over your flags while you run.
    • +
      Extra points:1 pt from the 3, 2 pts from the 10, 3 pts from the 20.
