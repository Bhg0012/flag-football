# Notes for working on this playbook

- The audience has little or no football experience. Keep plays, wording and UI as simple as possible.
- The defense can rush the QB the moment the ball is snapped, and usually sends several rushers. Favor quick throws. Every pass play and any play where the QB holds the ball (fakes, bootlegs) needs at least 2 blockers in front of the QB. Blocking is screen-only under league rules: stand in the rusher's path, hands behind the back, no contact.
- Rules come from the Co-Rec Flag Football Rules Guide: 4+ women on the field, only women run the ball past the line, no back-to-back man-to-man completions, a woman throwing or scoring a TD is worth 9.
- `index.html` is the whole app. The published Claude artifact is the same page without the `<html>`/`<head>`/`<body>` wrapper.
